Canvas help

Choose a color, then double-click or double-tap a pixel to paint it. Turn on Single click to paint as soon as you press. Use Pan to move without painting. The first placement starts the timer. All placements refill together.

Drag to pan. Drag the Navigator thumbnail to move around the board. Scroll, pinch, or use the slider to zoom. Fit shows all nine million pixels.

With the canvas focused, use the arrow keys to select a pixel and Enter to place it.
